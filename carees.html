AI

What is AI?

AI is the simulation of human intelligence by machines.

What is machine learning?

Machine learning is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.

Where is AI used today?

AI is used in healthcare, finance, transportation, and virtual assistants.

Can AI think like humans?

No, AI mimics certain tasks but lacks true human consciousness or emotions.

What is the future of AI?

The future of AI involves smarter automation, ethical concerns, and enhanced human-AI collaboration.
